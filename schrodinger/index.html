Schrödinger Playground

$$ i \hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\Psi(\mathbf{r},t) = \left( -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2 + V(\mathbf{r}) \right)\Psi(\mathbf{r},t) $$
Explore 2D Schrödinger-equation dynamics through interactive simulations of Gaussian wave packets on customizable potential landscapes. Simulations rely on the Crank–Nicolson method for stability and use a Patra-Karttunen 9-point Laplacian stencil for accuracy. For Eigenstate construction, we use imaginary-time evolution with Gram–Schmidt.
